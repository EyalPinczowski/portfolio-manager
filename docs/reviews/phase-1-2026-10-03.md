## Risks for Phase 1

1. A 1 GB VPS can't run `next build`, Next.js, FastAPI with pandas, and Tesseract together. Build the images in CI, add swap, and use a 2 GB plan or a static-export frontend.
2. APScheduler with several uvicorn workers runs every job more than once. Use a separate scheduler process, and SQLite in WAL mode.
3. Hebrew right-to-left OCR gets numbers, signs and units wrong. Use a schema-constrained prompt, check qty × price ≈ value, and highlight failing rows.
4. Yahoo may throttle a datacenter IP. Serve stale cache with an "as of" time, and use Finnhub quotes or CoinGecko as a fallback.
5. Dual listings get double-counted unless the mapping exists in Phase 1.
6. The Gemini quota or policy may change. Tesseract must be a working path from day 1.
7. Scope creep: limit the PWA to a manifest and an offline shell, with no push yet.
8. Legal and privacy exposure if people are invited before the disclaimer, consent and encryption are in place.

## Recommended changes to the Phase 1 plan

1. Replace pandas-ta with TA-Lib or in-house indicators.
2. Add a `security_master` table, seeded from TASE Data Hub.
3. Normalize agorot by the `currency` field.
4. Data model: HoldingsSnapshot, inferred Transactions, CashBalance, manual-price holdings, daily PortfolioSnapshot, Bank of Israel FX.
5. Show scores, not verdicts. Enforce the backtest gate on the server. Start the forward-test log.
6. Risk: RiskFilter, presets, overrides and exposure math. Stop-based rules move to Phase 2.
7. The auth hardening listed in problem 9, encrypted backups, and deleting screenshots after confirmation.
8. Screenshot pipeline: local redaction, then Gemini with a JSON schema, with Tesseract as fallback. Validate rows and record consent.
9. A separate scheduler, per-asset-class jobs, a TASE calendar override, and batched quotes.
10. Build in CI, swap, a 2 GB VPS or static export, and a queued Tesseract worker.
11. A disclaimer accepted at signup, export/delete, and a legal-opinion note.
12. Live updates in Phase 1 by polling (60 s); the WebSocket comes later.
